## Application Description
 
Budget Base is a detailed expense tracker able to record different kinds of transactions between income and expenses and categorize them based on type. Budget Base offers users multiple budgeting plans alongside detailed lists and general overviews so that users may understand their finances at a micro and macro level while checking in on their budget goal progress.
 
Users who need to manage their finances and wish to begin budgeting in a simple-to-use manner will be able to understand and control their expenses through Budget Base. Due to the nature of Budget Base's mobile architecture, users will be able to quickly check progress anytime, and may verify budgeting needs before any transaction conveniently. The primary goal of Budget Base is to make tracking income and expenses, as well as creating financial goals, easy and accessible for everyone.
 
## Major Features
 
- Add and categorize expenses
- View transaction list in detail
- View overview/summary of transactions
- Choose budgeting plan
- View progress of budgeting goals
## Mobile Device Capabilities
 
**Notifications**
 
- Give user low balance warnings
- Remind user of recurring bills
- Alert user when spending threshold is approached
